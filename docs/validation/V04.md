The input conflict is resolved technically: aiming does not request supporting
arm length, effort or posture changes. Keep the opt-in experiment for human
comparison; do not promote automatic pulling to final controls. Precision,
agency, fatigue and enjoyment have not been certified by a human session.

## M2 / bounded support plans

New `support_posture_planner.gd` computes an absolute material-anchor configuration
and conservative body frame from real support normals and anatomy. A holds
initial length. B weights supports equally; C weights estimated capacity and
vertical usefulness, clamped to 20?80%. Cached configurations cannot accumulate
height by regrabbing identical points. This cache resets with the trial.

New `arm_target_coordinator.gd` rate- and acceleration-limits existing actuator
targets. It waits for grip blend, brakes on slip, saturation, tracking stall,
infeasible geometry or deadline, then stops advancing. COMPLETE means arm
targets were achieved, not that the full body pose was achieved. Body pose error
and actual movement remain visible. No body transform/velocity writes were added.

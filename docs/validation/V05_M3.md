# v0.5 M3 — supported swing and actual capture

The existing supported swing can deliberately pump and brake motion and create a real catch opportunity. No additional propulsion or handoff rewrite was needed. The single-point grip remains a finite torque-capable gripper approximation, with paired reactions on dynamic supports; it is not an ideal moment-free joint.

The separate scene06 begins with the far underside contact about.884m from the free shoulder, beyond the.65708m anatomical limit. `test_transfer_swing` uses actual pointer/button input and phase-timed A/D, then one deliberate right-hand press once the real shoulder enters range. P0/P1/P2 all catch with about.30s finite travel and retain the new support after releasing the old hand. Incoming body speed is about.81m/s at press and.28m/s at contact. Sampled peaks stay around221N/43Nm; pressing does not write body pose or velocity. Release removes climbing actuation.

The initial attempt aimed at a front face while the shoulder was behind it. It failed `PATH_OBSTRUCTED`. Its raw report remains in `v05_m3/adverse/front_face_from_behind_120hz.json`. Aiming at the physically accessible underside solved the fixture without altering strength, reach or geometry. Arbitrary pointer locations therefore remain fallible, as intended.

Read-only diagnostics now separate incremental swing power/work and `PUMPING`, `BRAKING`, `NEUTRAL` phases from axial and total gripper work. Sign is measured from actual capped incremental moment against support-relative angular speed; it is not a claim about player intention or complete mechanical energy. Tab exposes the phase and accumulated estimate.

`test_transfer_swing` passes24/24 at60/120Hz. `test_swing_diagnostics` passes67/67 at each frequency, including P0/P1/P2 pump/coast/brake and twelve-second constant single/dual-grip holds. Late speed stays at most.062m/s single and.0038m/s dual in those fixtures; no persistent jetpack behavior was observed. P0/P1 pump work is+7.835/+8.449J and brake−2.099/−2.193J at120/60Hz.
